BeeYarn Campus Ambassador

Open to all students · Any Nigerian higher institution
Fully Remote, Nigeria  ·  Flexible hours  ·  Performance-based earnings

About BeeYarn
BeeYarn is a Nigerian-built social platform for real conversations, communities, and creators. Think Twitter/X — but made for us. We're building a nationwide community and we want passionate students from every higher institution in Nigeria to help us grow it from the ground up.

Who can apply

Any current student of a Nigerian university, polytechnic, or college of education. No experience needed. No follower count required. All applications are welcome and reviewed.

What you'll do

  • Represent BeeYarn as the official student voice at your institution.
  • Promote BeeYarn across campus — social media, WhatsApp groups, campus events, and word of mouth.
  • Drive sign-ups at your institution using your unique BeeYarn referral link.
  • Create and engage with content on BeeYarn to grow your presence and earn Yarn Points.
  • Be creative — flyers, memes, campus shoutouts, association partnerships — you know your campus best.
  • Feed insights and feedback from your campus community back to the BeeYarn team.

What's in it for you

  • Earn from your hustle — referrals and platform activity earn you Yarn Points, which can be converted to Naira once you meet monetisation requirements.
  • Official Ambassador Certificate — a verified BeeYarn certificate to strengthen your CV.
  • PDF recommendation letter — a written reference from the BeeYarn team, available on request.
  • Real startup experience — hands-on growth marketing experience at a live, scaling Nigerian tech product.
  • National Ambassador network — connect with fellow ambassadors from institutions across Nigeria.

BeeYarn is proudly Nigerian-built. Let's take it to every campus in the country — starting with yours.
