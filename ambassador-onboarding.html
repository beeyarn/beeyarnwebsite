Congratulations!

Your application to become a BeeYarn Campus Ambassador has been reviewed and accepted. Welcome to the team. You are now an official representative of BeeYarn at your institution and part of a growing network of student ambassadors across Africa.

This guide walks you through everything you need to get started, from installing the app to earning and cashing out.

Step 1

Get the app

BeeYarn is available as a native app on Android, and as a web app for everyone else.

  • Create your account using your email address.
  • Set up your profile: use a clear photo and a bio that represents you well. You are now the face of BeeYarn on your campus.
Download on Google Play

On iPhone? The native iOS app is on its way, in the meantime, use BeeYarn on the web at beeyarn.com/home.

Step 2

Get your unique share link

Your share link is how BeeYarn tracks every sign-up you refer. Every person who creates a BeeYarn account through your link is credited to you.

To get your share link:

Profile → Settings → Refer & Earn → Copy your share link

Keep this link somewhere safe. This is your most important tool as an ambassador.

Step 3

Start referring

Share your link everywhere across your campus:

  • WhatsApp groups (class groups, association groups, hostel groups)
  • Your Instagram, X (Twitter), TikTok, Facebook stories and posts
  • Campus notice boards (print a flyer with your link or a QR code)
  • In person: tell people directly and send them your link
  • Campus radio, departmental events, orientation programmes
  • Student union, clubs, and association platforms

The more people sign up through your link, the more Yarn Points you earn. There is no cap.

Step 4

Stay active on BeeYarn

Referring people is just one part of the role. You also need to be an active and visible presence on the platform itself. Points are earned through:

  • Creating posts
  • Engaging with other people's posts (likes, comments)
  • Sharing posts
  • Referring new users via your share link
  • Daily platform activity

The more active you are, the faster your Yarn Points grow.

Step 5

Unlock monetisation

Once you have built your presence and accumulated points, you can convert your Yarn Points to cash. To unlock monetisation, you must meet all four of the following requirements:

Requirement 1: Verify your identity

Settings → Verify Identity

Submit your identity documents for verification. This is processed by the BeeYarn admin team and typically takes a few days. Start this early. It must be completed before you can cash out.

Requirement 2: Reach 10,000 followers

You must reach 10,000 followers on BeeYarn before monetisation is unlocked. Grow your following by posting consistently, engaging with others, and encouraging your referrals to follow you back.

Requirement 3: Maintain 3 consecutive months of subscription

Settings → Subscription → Subscribe

You must hold an active BeeYarn subscription for 3 consecutive months.

Important: Enable auto-renewal to avoid your streak being broken by a missed payment. A lapsed subscription resets your consecutive count.

Requirement 4: Accumulate 10,000 Yarn Points

You must accumulate 10,000 Yarn Points before you can convert to cash. Your points build up through referrals and daily activity, keep going.

Once all four requirements are met, you will be able to convert your Yarn Points to cash directly within the app.

Rules & Expectations

What we expect from you

  • Represent BeeYarn professionally and positively at all times.
  • Comply with BeeYarn's Content Policy and Terms of Use at all times.
  • Do not make false claims about BeeYarn, its features, or earning potential when recruiting.
  • Do not use spam, bots, or any artificial means to inflate your referrals or points. This will result in immediate removal from the programme and forfeiture of accumulated points.
  • Remain active on the platform. Ambassadors who go inactive for an extended period may be removed from the programme.
