BeeYarn Content Policy

Effective Date: April 19, 2026

BeeYarn is a social platform built for community discussion, discovery, and connection. This policy outlines what is and is not allowed on the platform.

1. Prohibited Content

The following content is not allowed on BeeYarn:

Hate Speech

Content that attacks, dehumanises, or promotes hatred against individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin.

Violence & Dangerous Content

Threats of violence, content that glorifies harm, or material that could incite real-world violence against individuals or groups.

Harassment & Bullying

Targeted harassment, sustained abuse, or coordinated campaigns intended to intimidate or harm another user.

Nudity & Sexual Content

Explicit sexual content or nudity. Content involving minors in any sexual context is strictly prohibited and will be reported to the relevant authorities.

Misinformation & Fake News

Content that deliberately spreads false information that could cause public harm, including medical misinformation, fabricated news, or manipulated media presented as fact.

Scams & Fraud

Deceptive content, phishing attempts, impersonation of other users or organisations, pyramid schemes, or any form of financial fraud.

Spam

Repetitive, automated, or unsolicited content designed to manipulate visibility or deceive users.

Copyright Violations

Content that infringes on the intellectual property rights of others without permission or a valid fair use basis.

2. Advertising Standards

Ads on BeeYarn must comply with the following:

  • Ad content must be accurate and not misleading
  • Advertisers must honour any claims made in their ads
  • Ads may not promote prohibited content categories listed in Section 1
  • Ads targeting specific demographics must comply with applicable laws
  • WhatsApp numbers and destination URLs submitted with an ad must be legitimate, functional, and directly related to the advertised product or service
  • Sponsored content must be clearly distinguishable as advertising
  • Comments on ads may be enabled or disabled at the advertiser's discretion; BeeYarn does not guarantee comment availability on any ad

BeeYarn reviews all ads before they go live. Ads that do not meet this policy will be rejected with a stated reason. BeeYarn reserves the right to reject or remove any ad at any time, including after a campaign has started, without refund if the content violates this policy.

Ad campaigns that are cancelled early by the advertiser after going live are not eligible for a refund of the campaign fee.

3. Reporting

Users can report posts, comments, and ads for the following reasons:

  • Spam
  • Misinformation / Fake News
  • Hate Speech
  • Nudity or Sexual Content
  • Violence or Dangerous Content
  • Harassment or Bullying
  • Scam or Fraud
  • Copyright Violation
  • Other

Reports are reviewed by the BeeYarn moderation team. Repeated or egregious violations may result in account suspension or permanent removal from the platform.

4. Commenting

Post authors and advertisers may enable or disable comments on their content. BeeYarn does not guarantee that comments will remain active on any given post or ad. Comment sections are subject to the same content standards as posts.

5. Blocking

Users may block other users at any time. Blocking is mutual in effect — blocked users cannot view your profile or interact with your content, and their posts will not appear in your feed. BeeYarn enforces blocks at the content level across all platform surfaces.

6. Monetisation & Payouts

BeeYarn offers a Yarn Points system that rewards eligible users for platform activity. Payout requests are subject to identity and bank account verification before they can be processed. BeeYarn reserves the right to withhold payouts where there is evidence of fraudulent activity, policy violations, or unresolved disputes.

7. Your Data

You may request an export of your personal data at any time through the platform. BeeYarn will process your request and make your data available for download. For data deletion or other privacy requests, contact support.

8. Enforcement

BeeYarn may take the following actions in response to policy violations:

  • Content removal — the post, comment, or ad is taken down
  • Ad rejection — the ad is denied during review, with a reason provided
  • Campaign termination — an active ad campaign is ended early without refund
  • Account suspension — temporary restriction of account access
  • Account termination — permanent removal from the platform

For serious violations (e.g. child safety, credible threats of violence), BeeYarn will cooperate with law enforcement.

9. Appeals

If you believe your content was removed or your account actioned in error, contact BeeYarn support at support@beeyarn.com. We review appeals on a case-by-case basis.

10. Changes to This Policy

BeeYarn may update this policy at any time. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
