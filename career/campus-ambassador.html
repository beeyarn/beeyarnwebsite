About the role

Be the face of BeeYarn on your campus

BeeYarn is a social platform built for people who want to be seen, be heard, and be rewarded. Post yarns, join communities, go live, and earn Yarn Points for every contribution you make, whether you're a creator, a reader, or just someone with something to say.

We're building something different: a platform that puts value back into the hands of its users. Proudly African-built, growing fast, and coming to every campus on the continent.

BeeYarn is proudly African-built. Let's take it to every campus on the continent, starting with yours.

Who can apply

  • Any current student at a university, polytechnic, or college anywhere in Africa
  • Anyone living close to a higher institution or secondary school
  • No experience needed
  • No follower count required
  • Applications are always open, every submission is reviewed
