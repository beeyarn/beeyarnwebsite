What Are Creator Levels?

BeeYarn was built on one powerful belief: everyone who contributes to a community deserves to be recognised. The Creator Level System is how we honour your journey — from the moment you share your very first yarn to the day you become an African Superstar known around the world.

Every time you create, engage, and show up for the BeeYarn community, you earn Yarn Points. Those points fuel your creator level — a public badge of honour displayed right on your profile for everyone to see.

No algorithm burying your progress. No paying to boost your status. Your level is earned by your contribution — nothing else. Be Seen. Be Heard.

How It Works

Your creator level is determined by your total Yarn Points earned over your lifetime on BeeYarn. Unlike other platforms that reward only the top fraction of creators, BeeYarn rewards everyone who contributes — creators and community members alike.

Important to Know

Your level is based on total points earned — not your current balance. Withdrawing your earnings never reduces your level. Every point you earn counts permanently towards your creator journey.

What Earns You Yarn Points?

  • Creating and sharing content — posts, discussions, and yarns
  • Going live and engaging in real-time with your audience
  • Receiving likes, comments, and engagement on your content
  • Reading, watching, and engaging with other creators' content
  • Participating in discussions and community activities
  • Referring friends and growing the BeeYarn community
  • Showing up daily — consistent creators are rewarded

Why Creator Levels Matter

For too long, social media platforms have rewarded only the top fraction of creators while everyone else provides free labour. BeeYarn was built to flip that model completely.

Your creator level is more than a number — it is a public statement of your contribution to the BeeYarn community. It shows:

  • How consistently you show up and create
  • Your influence and impact within the community
  • Your standing as a creator on Africa's platform for the world

Whether you are in Lagos or London, Nairobi or New York — if you show up on BeeYarn, your journey is recognised. Every post, every yarn, every interaction moves you forward. No exceptions.
