02 / Principles

Built on a different model.

Traditional platforms extract value from creators and data from users, then store it far from the people who made it. BeeYarn inverts that.

Fair rewards
Everyone earns for engagementA 20-level creator system pays contributors, not only accounts with millions of followers.
Privacy first
User-controlled data, no surveillance advertisingDesigned by a privacy engineer. GDPR-aligned, with your data treated as yours.
Infrastructure
Self-hosted and locally resilientData hosted locally where possible, always within the Global South, not dependent on US big tech.
Made for
Emerging-market creator economiesPayouts in local currency, community-building and micro-monetisation across regions.
